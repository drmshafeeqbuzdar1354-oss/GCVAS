About the college

Built for practical excellence

Grace College of Veterinary and Animal Sciences & Vocational Institute is located in Choti Zareen, Nawan Shahir. Founded with a vision to empower students through hands-on education, our institute offers rigorous programs in veterinary healthcare, livestock management, and professional vocational arts.

"Practical training with real-world exposure is the cornerstone of career readiness."

Students rotate through active veterinary hospitals, dairy units, poultry farms, and modern vocational labs under direct supervision of experienced doctors and master instructors. Classroom theory is tightly integrated with practical field assignments from week one.

In addition to our core livestock and veterinary wing, our Creative & Vocational Skills wing provides accredited diplomas in Fashion Designing, Beautician & Salon Management, Video Production, Interior Design, and AutoCAD.
